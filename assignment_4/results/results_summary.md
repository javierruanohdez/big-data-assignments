## Key results

- Strong scaling, n = 2000: fastest at p = 4 with 0.697 s, S = 1.47 (E = 37 %); at p = 8: S = 1.32 (E = 17 %). Against NumPy in one process (same tiles and kernel, no Dask): 0.93x at p = 4, 0.63x at p = 1.
- Strong scaling, n = 4000: fastest at p = 4 with 3.686 s, S = 2.08 (E = 52 %); at p = 8: S = 1.88 (E = 23 %). Against NumPy in one process (same tiles and kernel, no Dask): 1.10x at p = 4, 0.53x at p = 1.
- Weak scaling: T = 0.411 s at p = 1 (n = 1000) and 0.797 s at p = 8 (n = 2000): E_w = 52 %. Data moved between workers: 0 MB -> 128.0 MB.
- Cross-paradigm, n = 2000: C sequential 13.67 s; C OpenMP best 1.074 s at p = 8 (12.7x); Dask (distributed) best 0.697 s at p = 4 (19.6x); NumPy, 1 process 0.648 s (21.1x).
- Cluster setup (one-time, not in e2e): 10.7 s to 15.5 s at n = 4000.
